What a class looks like

  • Partner dancing

    We rotate partners during class, so you quickly get to know the whole group, and you don't need to bring a partner.

  • Step by step

    We work on rhythm, basic steps, leading and following, and then on figures.

  • No pressure

    We want our classes to be a place where you progress at your own pace, ask questions and look forward to the next one.

  • Practical skills

    You don't learn figures to memorize them, but to be able to dance with anyone at a party. With every class your confidence grows, on the dance floor and off it.

FAQ

Do I need a partner?

No. Most students come on their own, and we rotate partners during class, so everyone always has someone to dance with.

Do you take beginners?

We open new groups, including groups for beginners, from time to time. We announce enrollment on Instagram, so follow @anitainikola.

Can we come as a couple?

Of course, couples are welcome. We rotate partners during class, so you will dance with each other and with the rest of the group.

Who can join?

Classes are for adults, 18 and over. Students also have a shared Viber group.

How much does it cost?

The monthly membership is 2,000 RSD for 8 classes, paid per calendar month. A single class is 500 RSD.

How do I sign up?

Send us a message on Instagram at @anitainikola and wait for us to confirm your spot.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move freely in, and a spare pair of sneakers that you only wear in the studio.
